Abstract & Executive Summary
Traditional automobile insurance pricing relies on demographic proxies (age, gender, postal code, credit history) that penalize safe drivers and fail to capture actual driving risk. We present INSURE, an actuarially calibrated, privacy-preserving IoT telematics platform. INSURE ingests real-world smartphone and OBD-II vehicle telemetry with uncoerced consent, defensively validates noisy sensor records, calculates deterministic risk scores across 5 physical driving factors, computes transparent quotes with itemized savings, and actively audits demographic fairness. Under the Section 8 growth benchmark, this model lowers policyholder premiums by 10% while increasing net insurer underwriting profit by +63.33% through adverse selection elimination.
